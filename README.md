# Job Portal MOI Brands

An AI-powered recruitment platform designed to streamline the hiring process at MOI Brands by helping HR teams identify candidates who meet job requirements more efficiently.

## About the Project

**Job Portal MOI Brands** is a capstone project developed as part of the **Asah by Dicoding** program in collaboration with MOI Brands, a food and beverage (F&B) business operating across multiple brands.

The platform addresses a key recruitment challenge: HR teams repeatedly spend time screening applications from candidates who do not meet the required qualifications for a position.

By introducing automated candidate screening and AI-assisted CV evaluation, the platform aims to reduce repetitive manual work, improve recruitment efficiency, and help HR focus on more qualified candidates.

## Core Features

### 1. Automated Candidate Screening

Automatically evaluates submitted applications against predefined job requirements and qualification criteria.

- Filters candidates based on job-specific requirements.
- Helps HR avoid repeatedly screening applications from candidates who do not meet the criteria.
- Organizes applicants based on their screening results.
- Helps HR prioritize candidates who are more suitable for further evaluation.

### 2. AI-Powered CV Rating

Provides an AI-assisted assessment of CVs based on the requirements of a specific job vacancy.

- Evaluates the relevance of a candidate's CV to the job description.
- Generates a rating or score based on defined evaluation criteria.
- Provides supporting insights to help HR understand candidate suitability.
- Assists HR in making more informed screening decisions.

*AI-generated ratings are intended to support HR decision-making, not replace human judgment.*

## Project Objectives

- Reduce repetitive manual CV screening.
- Improve the efficiency of the recruitment process.
- Help HR identify candidates who meet job requirements.
- Provide AI-assisted insights for evaluating candidate qualifications.
- Support a more structured and consistent recruitment workflow.

## Target Users

- **HR Team:** Manage job vacancies, review applications, and evaluate candidates more efficiently.
- **Job Applicants:** Submit applications and provide their qualifications for consideration.
